# GSU Parking Policy Proposal - Executive Summary for Decision Makers

## Quick Facts

- **Problem:** 81% of GSU students (42,120 students) live off-campus and lack access to affordable parking
- **Current Cost:** Campus-adjacent students pay $600-$800/semester vs. $400 for on-campus residents
- **Cost Disparity:** 50-100% price premium for the majority of student population
- **Demand:** 72% of surveyed adjacent students want overnight parking; 56-66% willing to pay $350-$450/semester
- **Opportunity:** Underutilized parking facilities could generate $20,000+ annually while serving students

---

## The Problem in 3 Points

### 1. Inequitable Access
- Campus-adjacent students have **zero options** to park on GSU facilities
- 24-hour lots restricted to on-campus residents only
- Overnight parking strictly prohibited (cars will be towed)
- Creates two-tier system: haves (on-campus) and have-nots (adjacent)

### 2. Financial Burden
- Adjacent students pay **$200-400 MORE per semester** than on-campus peers
- Some students opt out of vehicle ownership due to cost
- Reduces job flexibility and career opportunities
- Contributes to overall cost of attendance, reducing access to higher education

### 3. Operational Inefficiency
- GSU parking facilities sit empty after hours
- Campus loses potential revenue while operating costs continue
- Missed opportunity to serve growing off-campus population
- Underutilized infrastructure represents wasted institutional investment

---

## The Proposed Solution

### Two Core Changes:

**1. Enable Overnight Parking**
- Allow campus-adjacent students to park overnight in designated facilities
- Implement safety measures (lighting, security cameras, enforcement)
- Address needs of students studying late or requiring extended campus access

**2. Create On-Campus Parking Program for Adjacent Students**
- Offer semester permits in designated, underutilized lots
- Price at $350-$400 per semester (market rate: $600-$800)
- Reduce cost burden while generating institutional revenue
- Target lots near dorms not at full capacity

**3. Partner with Off-Campus Property Managers (Optional)**
- Negotiate discounted parking rates at local apartments
- Win-win: property occupancy increases, students save money
- Strengthens university-community relationships

## Supporting Evidence

### Survey Data (Campus-Adjacent Students)

**Question: Would you want GSU to provide overnight parking?**
- Yes: 72%
- No: 28%

**Question: How much would you pay per semester for on-campus parking?**
- $350-$450: 56-66% of respondents
- Majority concentration indicates strong pricing sweet spot

**Question: Are you satisfied with current parking policies?**
- Yes: 8%
- No: 92%

**Insight:** Clear, demonstrated demand with quantified willingness to pay
