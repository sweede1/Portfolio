# GSU Parking Policy Proposal - Implementation Roadmap

## Executive Summary
This document outlines a strategic implementation plan for inclusive parking policies at Georgia State University, specifically targeting campus-adjacent students.

---

## Phase 1: Assessment & Stakeholder Engagement (Months 1-2)

### 1.1 Establish Task Force
- [ ] Parking and Transportation Department
- [ ] Facilities and Operations
- [ ] Student Life and Housing
- [ ] Finance and Budget
- [ ] Student representatives (on-campus and adjacent)

### 1.2 Infrastructure Audit
- [ ] Assess current parking capacity across all lots and decks
- [ ] Identify underutilized facilities, especially after-hours
- [ ] Analyze maintenance and operational costs
- [ ] Review security systems and overnight monitoring capabilities

### 1.3 Stakeholder Consultation
- [ ] Meet with campus-adjacent student representatives
- [ ] Consult with on-campus student government
- [ ] Engage with off-campus property management
- [ ] Interview Parking and Transportation staff

### 1.4 Regulatory Review
- [ ] Review current parking policies and contracts
- [ ] Check lease agreements with external partners
- [ ] Assess liability and insurance implications
- [ ] Evaluate ADA compliance requirements

## Risk Management

### Potential Risks & Mitigation Strategies

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|----------|
| Low uptake/demand | Revenue shortfall | Pre-launch survey validation; aggressive marketing |
| Technical system failures | Permit/revenue tracking issues | Redundant systems; regular maintenance |
| Safety/security concerns | Liability; student trust | Enhanced lighting; security cameras; enforcement |
| Conflict with on-campus students | Community division | Transparent communication; equitable policies |
| Operational cost overruns | Budget constraints | Detailed cost analysis; contingency planning |
| Space availability conflicts | Capacity issues | Real-time monitoring; flexible allocation |

## Budget Outline (Year 1)

### Capital Costs
- Technology/Software: $15,000-25,000
- Infrastructure upgrades: $20,000-30,000
- Signage and marking: $5,000-10,000
- **Total Capital:** $40,000-65,000

### Operating Costs
- Additional staff/overtime: $30,000-50,000
- Maintenance and utilities: $10,000-15,000
- Software licensing: $5,000-8,000
- Marketing/communications: $5,000-10,000
- **Total Operating:** $50,000-83,000

### Expected Revenue (Conservative)
- 50 permits × $400/semester × 2 semesters = $40,000
- Off-peak parking contracts: $10,000-15,000
- **Total Revenue:** $50,000-55,000

### Net Impact (Year 1)
Estimated break-even to modest surplus, with significant student benefit (cost savings: $14.6M for student population)
